[h1]开局一键爽玩 | One-Click Sandbox Start[/h1]

不想每次开局都重复输入控制台、研究科技学说、配置军工机构、重建设计编制，再逐地块安排工厂、机场、基建、炼油厂、船坞建设？

本MOD面向[b]所有非AI玩家国家[/b]，通过零政治点决议快速完成沙盒开局——但绝不会超过正常游戏过程中能达到的程度，拒绝魔改！

[h2]一键开局初始化[/h2]
[list]
[*]完成全部科技以及预设的陆军、海军、空军和特种部队学说
[*]合理配置8项军种精神
[*]为当前国家实际拥有的军工机构合理配置MIO特质路线——不然每次开局那十来个MIO都要点无数次！
[*]创建22个陆空装备设计和8个师级编制
[*]补充政治点、指挥点、三军经验，以及巨量陆空装备和满级变体
[/list]

初始化[b]不会自动改编现有部队[/b]。需要时可另行执行决议，将当前国家全部现役陆军统一改编为“卫戍部队”。

[h2]编制、部队与库存[/h2]
[list]
[*]可重复组建24个满人力、满装备、满初始训练度的“主力纯步师”
[*]可重复补充战略装备库存
[*]完成相关特殊科研后，可创建“司令部”“装甲师·进化型”“海军陆战队装甲师·进化型”和“主力步坦师·进化型”
[*]同时创建最终中型喷火坦克、全5升级改进型履带登陆车、4种装甲支援车和4种特殊轰炸机设计
[*]通用国家使用中性装备名称和普通步兵编制；
[/list]

[*]完成奖励包括对应科技、装备、模块解锁和首枚免费核弹

[h2]全国建设[/h2]

建设仅处理[b]当前国家拥有且控制的州[/b]，不会改造临时占领的敌国州。

[list]
[*]将共享建设槽解锁至原版上限25
[*]补足5级基础设施、10级空军基地和10级已有海军基地
[*]补足5级州防空，并在各省建设10级陆上要塞和海岸要塞
[*]各州合成炼油厂和储油罐分别补足至3座
[*]沿海州最多建设5座海军船坞，各州最多建设5座民用工厂
[*]槽位不足时保留部分进度，释放槽位后可再次执行
[/list]

部分国家拥有额外的专属建设分支；这些工程只会在适用国家执行。

[h1]English Description[/h1]

Tired of repeating console commands, research, MIO traits, equipment designs, division templates, and nationwide construction every campaign?

This mod gives [b]every non-AI player country[/b] zero-cost decisions for a fast sandbox start, while staying within results obtainable through normal gameplay.

[h2]One-Click Initialization[/h2]
[list]
[*]Completes all technologies and preset Army, Navy, Air, and Special Forces doctrines
[*]Assigns 8 officer corps spirits
[*]Completes sensible trait routes for MIOs actually owned by the current country; no extra MIO funds or levels are added, and policies remain player-selected
[*]Creates 22 land/air equipment designs and 8 powerful 36-width division templates
[*]Provides political power, command power, military experience, and large equipment stockpiles
[/list]

Initialization [b]does not automatically convert existing divisions[/b]. A separate decision can convert the current army to the Garrison template.

[h2]Forces, Designs, and Projects[/h2]
[list]
[*]Repeatably deploys 24 fully manned, equipped, and trained Main Infantry Divisions
[*]Repeatably replenishes strategic equipment stockpiles
[*]After the required special projects, creates evolved HQ, Armored, Marine Armored, and Main Infantry-Tank templates
[*]Also creates a final medium flame tank, fully upgraded improved amphibious mechanized vehicle, 4 armored support vehicles, and 4 special bomber designs
[*]Completes all 49 special projects across Air/Radar, Land, Naval, Nuclear, and Rocket specializations, including their technologies, unlocks, and the first free nuclear bomb
[/list]

Special projects require the [i]Gotterdammerung[/i] DLC.

[h2]Nationwide Construction[/h2]

Construction affects only states [b]owned and controlled by the current country[/b], never temporarily occupied enemy states.

[list]
[*]Unlocks shared building slots to the vanilla cap of 25
[*]Raises infrastructure to 5, air bases to 10, existing naval bases to 10, and state AA to 5
[*]Builds level-10 land and coastal forts in provinces
[*]Raises synthetic refineries and fuel silos to 3 per state
[*]Builds up to 5 dockyards in coastal states and 5 civilian factories per state
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*; designed with all DLC enabled
[*]Supports puppets, civil-war countries, dynamic countries, and switching player countries within one campaign
[*]All decisions cost 0 political power and cannot be used by AI
[*]Does not overwrite vanilla focus trees, country history, or event chains
[*]Does not create ship designs or fleets
[*]Avoid combining it with other large mods that overhaul technology, equipment, templates, MIOs, or construction
[/list]
