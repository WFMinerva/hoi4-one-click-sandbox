[h1]开局一键爽玩 | One-Click Sandbox Start[/h1]

不想每次开局都重复输入控制台、研究科技学说、重建设计编制，再逐州安排工业建设？

本MOD为[b]全国家通用版[/b]。所有非AI玩家国家均可使用零成本决议快速完成沙盒开局，并可一键完成五类特殊科研项目（需《Gotterdammerung》DLC）；PRC继续保留顾问、军工机构、中国型号、固定补给站和双电网等专属内容。

[h2]一键开局[/h2]
[list]
[*]完成全部科技、预设陆海空及特种部队学说，并配置8项军种精神
[*]强化本国现有军工机构，创建22个装备设计和8个师级编制
[*]补充政治点、指挥点、三军经验，以及37类陆空装备和预设变体各100万件
[*]初始化不会修改现有部队；可另行将全军改编为“卫戍部队”
[*]可一次性组建24个满人力、满装备、满训练度的“主力纯步师”
[*]可重复补充战略装备库存
[/list]

PRC使用中国型号和民兵编制，并保留顾问、军事高层及4家军工机构预设特质；其他国家使用中性型号和普通步兵编制。

[h2]特殊科研项目（需《Gotterdammerung》DLC）[/h2]
[list]
[*]按空军（含雷达）、陆军、海军、核能、火箭五类专精，一键完成全部49个特殊科研项目
[*]完成奖励（科技、装备与模块解锁、首枚免费核弹）与正常研究完全一致
[*]直接授予57项衍生科技与原型迭代互斥选择科技（含离心法同位素分离、海军补给与维修方案）
[*]核能决议在首都额外补建1座核反应堆
[*]决议可重复执行：跨专精前置被跳过时，完成其他专精后再次点击即可补齐；全部完成后决议自动消失
[/list]

[h2]全国建设[/h2]

建设仅作用于[b]本国拥有且控制的州[/b]，不会处理临时占领区。

[list]
[*]共享建设槽解锁至原版上限25
[*]基础设施5级、机场10级、已有港口10级
[*]州防空5级，各省陆地与海岸要塞10级
[*]各州炼油厂和储油罐分别补足至3座
[*]沿海州最多5座船坞，各州最多5座民用工厂

工业优先级：
[b]炼油厂 → 储油罐 → 船坞 → 民工 → PRC电网[/b]

未完成项目会保留进度，可在取得新领土或释放槽位后再次执行。

[h1]English Description[/h1]

This mod is [b]universal for all non-AI player countries[/b]. Zero-cost decisions provide a complete sandbox setup, including one-click completion of all five special research project trees (requires the Gotterdammerung DLC). The PRC retains additional advisers, MIO paths, Chinese designs, preset supply hubs and its dual power-grid system.

[h2]One-Click Setup[/h2]
[list]
[*]Completes all technologies, preset Army, Navy, Air and Special Forces doctrines, plus 8 officer corps spirits
[*]Strengthens existing national MIOs and creates 22 equipment designs and 8 division templates
[*]Adds political power, command power, military experience, and one million units of 37 supported land and air equipment entries
[*]Does not alter existing divisions; army-wide conversion to the Garrison template is optional
[*]Can raise 24 fully manned, equipped and trained Main Infantry Divisions
[*]Provides repeatable strategic stockpile resupply
[/list]

The PRC keeps Chinese designs, militia templates, advisers, military leadership and four preset MIO paths. Other countries receive neutral designs and regular-infantry templates.

[h2]Special Research Projects (requires Gotterdammerung)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Completes all 49 special research projects across five specializations: Air (including radar), Land, Naval, Nuclear and Rocket
[*]Completion rewards (technologies, equipment and module unlocks, the first free nuclear bomb) match normal research exactly
[*]Directly grants 57 derived and prototype-choice technologies (including centrifugal isotope separation and the naval support/repair variants)
[*]The nuclear decision additionally builds one nuclear reactor in the capital
[*]Re-runnable: projects skipped over missing cross-tree prerequisites can be picked up by clicking again; each decision disappears once its tree is complete
[/list]

[h2]Nationwide Construction[/h2]

Construction affects only states [b]owned and controlled by the current country[/b], excluding temporary occupations.

[list]
[*]Shared building slots unlocked to the vanilla cap of 25
[*]Infrastructure 5, air bases 10 and existing naval bases 10
[*]State AA 5 and land/coastal forts 10
[*]Synthetic refineries and fuel silos raised to 3 per state
[*]Up to 5 dockyards per coastal state and 5 civilian factories per state
[*]The PRC retains 23 preset supply hubs and its dual grid; other countries receive no power grids
[/list]

Priority: [b]Refineries → Fuel Silos → Dockyards → Civilian Factories → PRC Power Grid[/b]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Built for Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.2 / 1.19.* with all DLC (special project decisions require Gotterdammerung)
[*]Supports puppets, civil-war tags and dynamic countries
[*]All decisions cost 0 political power and are never used by the AI
[*]Does not replace vanilla focus trees, country history files or event chains
[*]Does not automatically build railways or create ship designs and fleets
[/list]
