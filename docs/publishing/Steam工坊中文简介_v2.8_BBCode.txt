[h1]English Description[/h1]

Zero-cost, player-only decisions for a fast single-player sandbox start — all within normal gameplay results.

[h2]New in v2.8[/h2]
[list]
[*]Intelligence agency upgrade gap fixed; supply/repair ship options show official tech effects
[*]No prehistoric jet techs/designs with the jet DLC; cruiser-submarine extra fuel tank unlocked
[*]All 42 prototype-reward options use official localization; nuclear graphite/heavy-water choice added
[*]Land-cruiser/super-heavy howitzer/naval/rocket equipment-bonus groups added, pickable one by one
[*]New decision: max out MIO funds and size; default MIO routes now pick the strongest builds
[*]9 positive generic land equipment bonuses granted with land special projects
[/list]

[h2]One-Click Initialization[/h2]
All technologies and doctrines; 8 service spirits; MIO trait routes; 22 designs, 8 templates, big stockpiles; special-project rewards picked one by one with concrete values.

[h2]Other Decisions[/h2]
Skull divisions (v2.7); occupied-territory development and supply-hub boost (v2.7); points/XP top-ups; nationwide construction (slots 25, infra 5, air bases 10, ports 10, AA 5, coastal forts 10, refineries/fuel to 3, dockyards/civ-s to cap); intelligence line (v2.5, La Resistance).

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.* (keep the latest hotfix); special projects need Gotterdammerung; AI never uses the decisions; no vanilla focus/history/event overwrites; avoid pairing with large overhaul mods.

Sincere thanks to an anonymous player for extensive in-game testing and feedback across v2.7–v2.8 — radar/intelligence fixes, special-project choice rework, supply-ship tooltips, cruiser-submarine and land-cruiser modules, MIO route direction, and official-localization alignment.
