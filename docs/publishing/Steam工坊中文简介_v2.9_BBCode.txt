[h1]English Description[/h1]

Player-only, zero-political-power decisions for a fast single-player sandbox start. The mod does not replace vanilla focus trees, history, or event chains, and the AI cannot use its decisions.

[h2]New in v2.9[/h2]
[list]
[*]Year-based research buttons; hidden legacy techs removed and missing no-year technologies restored
[*]New controls for stability, war support, autonomy, compliance, relations, ideology, laws, war goals, and special rules
[*]Preset designs separated from stockpiles; new carrier aircraft, tactical bomber, and five ship-design families
[*]Separate military-industry, power-grid, fortress, and large-facility tools; construction boost raised to 5000%
[*]Commercial reactors are preferred with Götterdämmerung; nuclear special projects no longer add a conflicting normal reactor
[*]Factory rebuild, autonomy/compliance errors, submarine MIO routes, and relation bonuses fixed
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*; Götterdämmerung is required for special projects and commercial reactors; La Resistance is required for intelligence features. Avoid combining with large overhaul mods.
